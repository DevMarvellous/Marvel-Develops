# Marvel Develops Brand Guidelines

## Our Logo
The Marvel Develops mark combines an ascending bar-chart 'M' with a checkmark accent — representing growth and completed, trustworthy work. Use the versions below depending on background.
- Mark — Transparent
- Mark — Dark background
- Mark — Light background
- Full Lockup — for use on light backgrounds
- Full Lockup — for use on dark backgrounds

### Clear Space & Don'ts
Always keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of one bar. Don't stretch, recolor, rotate, add drop shadows/bevels, or place the mark on busy photo backgrounds without a solid overlay panel behind it.

## Color Palette
- **Royal Blue** (`#2563EB`): Primary brand color — buttons, links, key accents
- **Purple** (`#7C3AED`): Secondary — gradients paired with Royal Blue
- **Gold / Amber** (`#F59E0B`): Accent — highlights, CTAs, the checkmark mark
- **Navy Deep** (`#0A0F1E`): Dark backgrounds, footer, dark-mode surfaces

**Gradient**: Royal Blue + Purple is the signature gradient, used on the 'MARVEL' wordmark and hero headings. Gold is used sparingly as a single accent — never as a dominant fill.

## Typography
To stay consistent with the live Marvel Develops website, keep using the same two-font system across every brand asset — flyers, the Academy page, and this guide's own layout follows it.
- **Clash Display**: Headings, logo wordmark, hero titles. Bold, geometric, confident.
- **Inter**: Body copy, forms, buttons, FAQ text. Clean and highly legible at small sizes.

## Voice & Tone
Professional, warm, concise, and jargon-free. Explain things in plain language — like talking to a friend, not a developer. Avoid technical jargon in customer-facing copy unless the audience is clearly technical.

For the Academy specifically: speak to outcomes ("build and deploy a real app") over features ("learn Node.js"). Parents and beginners respond to what they'll be able to do, not the tools used to do it.
